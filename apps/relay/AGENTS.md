# apps/relay — agent rules

Read the root [`AGENTS.md`](../../AGENTS.md) first. On-chain wire layouts and instruction tags are owned by `crates/audren-core/` and `program/`.

## Architectural boundaries

- **Standalone, relayer-agnostic service:** `apps/relay` runs as an independent Cloudflare Worker (`v1.3.0`) decoupled from `apps/web`. Any web client, mobile wallet, or backend SDK can target a hosted instance or self-host via `wrangler.jsonc` implementing `/v1/info`, `/v1/fee-quote`, `/v1/swap/quote`, `/v1/swap/instructions`, and `/v1/relay/submit`.
- **Zero-trust Audren v1.3.0 instruction firewall (`src/policy/firewall.ts`):**
  - Permit **only** Audren `Tag 5` (`Transact`, `380 B` fixed header — private transfer or public SPL/Token-2022/WSOL-unwrap withdraw, plus 1..=4 batched parallel `2 -> 1` private `Transact` merges capped at `<= 2` outputs/ix) and `Tag 6` (`ExecViaEscrow`, `560 B` fixed header — SPL/Token-2022 atomic DeFi CPI with optional `EXEC_FLAG_FEE_ON_OUTPUT`) targeting `AUDRBK6kVkQoEGm3EB7swd34SUPqrHZeNEkB5vZWVSv3` on Pool #0 (`audren_pool`, up to `96` instruction accounts matching Pinocchio while Solana v1 enforces `<= 64` unique accounts), enforcing `vk_id === 0`, valid dual-ring root bounds (`rootRingKind = 0` `< 128`, `rootRingKind = 1` `< 89`), canonical `GlobalRootHistory` (`audren_roots`, `249YJTuRtiKAnh346Tc18kF9bHrktdrTzyJMtvUKUgC7`) at `accounts[2]` with `isWritable: false` on full exits (`outputMask === 0`) and `isWritable: true` on non-zero outputs & `ExecViaEscrow`, trailing `Transact` accounts restricted strictly to Token-2022 non-unwrap public withdrawals, canonical ATAs, protocol-enforced on-chain `relayerFee > 0` for `ExecViaEscrow` self-pay credit, and `SELF_PAY_FEE_MINTS` + `parseMintSafetyInfo` for self-pay fee settlement, plus strictly allowlisted zero-signer `preInstructions` immediately preceding single `Tag 6`.
  - Block `rootsPda` (`audren_roots`) from appearing outside `accounts[2]` in `Transact` or `ExecViaEscrow`, or in `preInstructions` or `setupAtas`.
  - Explicitly reject `Tag 3` (`Deposit`) and `Tag 4` (`InitRootHistory`).
  - Require `accounts[0]` to match a configured relayer fee-payer keypair (`isSigner: true, isWritable: true`) and **zero** other signers across all accounts. Rotates advertised fee-payer pubkeys uniformly across configured `*_RELAYER_SECRETS_JSON` pools (accepting optional `relayerPubkey` pin on quote/instruction routes and any pool member as fee recipient) to distribute fee-payer SOL float and eliminate fee-payer write-lock contention on read-only full exits (`outputMask === 0`); note-appending `Transact` (`outputMask !== 0`) and `ExecViaEscrow` transactions serialize on the single pool-wide `GlobalRootHistory` write lock.
- **Zero-crank inline Auto-`OpenPage` & Calibrated Tight CU Limits (`src/index.ts`, `src/solana/audren.ts`):**
  - `planRolloverOpenPages` splits mandatory exact rollover `OpenPage` (`Tag 2`, `40_000` CU + `(240 - bump) * 1_500` when `bump < 240`, skipping all 5 note/memo page slots `outSub`/`outRoot`/`outMemo`/`nextSub`/`nextMemo` on `outputCount === 0` full exits) from optional proactive `>= 90%` lookahead (`subRem <= 26`, `memoRem <= 10`, `nfRem <= 60`, `subrootSlot >= 280`). Exact rollover lanes acquire mandatory `openPageLock` (returning `503 LANE_ROLLOVER_BUSY` only on exact rollover contention), while proactive-only lanes acquire locks opportunistically (dropping busy lanes cleanly). Proactive lookahead runs strictly when live rent is known (`rentSchedule !== null`), `LaneRentVault` covers 100% of full `pageRent` (`0n` deficit without subtracting un-pinned pre-funded lamports), and account (`<= 64`), wire-size (`<= 4,096 B`, `57 B`/page), and bump-aware CU (`<= 1,400,000`) caps permit; any wire overflow, post-pin proactive deficit, `sim.err`, or proactive-only `SLOT_SKEW` drops optional proactive pages, releases proactive-only locks, and recompiles with exact pages only (preserving `preCtxCommitment` on all proactive-drop retries and failing closed with `503 SLOT_SKEW` whenever `preCtx.slot === null || sim.slot === null`). Confirmed on-chain reverts (`TransactionFailedOnChainError`) charge `5_000 + priorityFeeLamports` against `daily_budgets` with `failed = 1` (counting toward per-IP `SPONSOR_COOLDOWN`, hourly, and daily limits on both sponsored and `selfPaying` requests, plus `DAILY_SPONSOR_BUDGET_REACHED` on `selfPaying` requests whenever `revertBurnsDay > 0 || hasUntrustedCpiTarget`, while excluding pre-sim `sponsored_lamports = 0` rejections and honest `failed = 0` sponsored txs), cap untrusted CPI targets (unaudited `ExecViaEscrow` routers, non-allowlisted Token-2022 `ExecViaEscrow` legs, and `Transact` `TransferHook` withdrawals) at `5_000` lamports priority fee, and release `open_page_locks` immediately (`graceTtlMs = 0`).
  - Compiles Solana Transaction v1 (`0x81`) with calibrated tight CU limits: `165_000` Full Exit (`outputMask === 0`, RO `roots`), `255_000` Withdraw (`outputMask !== 0`), `235_000` Private `Transact`, `295_000` Token-2022/Hook Withdraw, `N * 235_000` multi-ix batch, `285_000` base (`+ 25_000` when either leg is Token-2022) + router headroom (`350_000` single-hop direct / `550_000` multi-hop, OKX, or unknown CU) for `ExecViaEscrow`, plus `+ 40_000` per prepended `OpenPage` (plus bump-aware `(240 - bump) * 1_500` when `bump < 240`), `+ 30_000` per Classic SPL `setupAta`, and `+ 45_000` per Token-2022 `setupAta` (capped at `1_400_000`).
- **Three-mode dynamic relayer fee verification (`src/policy/firewall.ts`, `src/policy/pricing.ts`):**
  - Default `omitNf1 = false` (2 nullifiers) across ALL ops (`transfer`, `withdraw`, `exec_escrow`) and uniformly random default `shardId` (`0..15`) whenever omitted.
  - Mode 1 (`ASSET_MODE_PRIVATE` transfer, single or 1..=4 merge batch): shielded `cm2` fee note(s) (`106 B` versioned memos) verified cryptographically after `guard.begin()` via the hot Worker's read-only `FullViewingKey` (`*_RELAYER_FVK_JSON`, zero spend authority on hot infrastructure). Multi-ix batches unconditionally require `isSelfPaying === true`.
  - Mode 2 (`Transact` `ASSET_MODE_PUBLIC` Withdraw): `WITHDRAW_FLAG_UNWRAP_WSOL` lamports directly to a configured relayer pool pubkey, or SPL/Token-2022 to a canonical relayer pool ATA.
  - Mode 3 (`ExecViaEscrow`): SPL/Token-2022 input-leg fee or `EXEC_FLAG_FEE_ON_OUTPUT` output-leg fee verified via slot-pinned pre-flight `simulateV1Transaction` with deterministic `minExpectedCostLamports` floor, `SELF_PAY_MARGIN_BPS`, and active Token-2022 `TransferFeeConfig` haircut (`2x` on Mode 1 pre-receipt `cm2` notes; `1x` liquidation hop on Mode 2/3 post-receipt simulated ATA deltas).
- **Atomic persistent anti-abuse (`src/policy/guard-do.ts`):**
  - SQLite Durable Object `RelayGuard` keeps its ephemeral per-day random 32-byte secret IP-hash salt strictly in DO memory (never written to SQLite disk; auto-purged on UTC day rollover and via UTC midnight DO `alarm()`, using `audren/relay/ip-salt`), stores ZERO `ip_hash` on `inflight_nullifiers`, drops stale legacy tables cleanly on init, maintains per-lane `open_page_locks` (`45s` TTL) for concurrent inline `OpenPage` mutual exclusion, and enforces global daily sponsored SOL caps, per-IP limits, cooldowns, failed-attempt caps, and atomic in-flight nullifier deduplication.
- **Standardized aggregator adapters (`src/aggregators/`):**
  - Keep `JUPITER_CPI_EXCLUDED_DEXES` in `src/aggregators/jupiter.ts` synced with `crates/client/src/router.rs`, `packages/sdk/src/swap.ts`, and `apps/web/worker/index.ts`.
- **Self-sustaining treasury auto-refill cron (`src/cron/refill.ts`):**
  - Zero-crank cron (`*/2 * * * *`) runs exclusively `runAutoRefill` (`refillSinglePayer`), converting collected fee tokens from canonical relayer ATAs into native SOL float with a 95% oracle floor (on 1x net Token-2022 amount) and slot-pinned simulation.
- **Credentials & deployment hygiene:**
  - Read RPC URLs, relayer fee-payer secrets, read-only FVKs, and aggregator keys exclusively from Wrangler secrets or gitignored `.dev.vars`.
  - Keep `wrangler.jsonc` 100% open-source and self-hostable (`wrangler.prod.jsonc` is gitignored).
